# CampusQuery AI 🤖

An AI-powered college information chatbot that helps students
find information from college documents such as notices, PDFs,
circulars, academic documents, and other campus resources.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often need to search through multiple PDF files,
notices, and documents to find specific college information.

Finding information manually can be time-consuming and confusing.

CampusQuery AI aims to provide a simple conversational interface
where students can ask questions in natural language and receive
relevant answers from college documents.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

CampusQuery AI uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to:

1. Process college documents
2. Extract and split document content
3. Store searchable representations of the content
4. Retrieve relevant information based on the student's question
5. Generate an AI-powered answer
6. Show the source of the information

## 🏗️ Basic Architecture

Student
   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓
AI Model
